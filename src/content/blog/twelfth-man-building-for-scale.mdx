Lumen Field once hit 137.6 decibels of crowd noise. That's not a metaphor for load, that IS load: a sudden, coordinated spike that either your defense handles or it doesn't.

## The snap count is your rate limiter

A false start is what happens when you react to noise instead of signal. Rate limiters and circuit breakers are your offensive line staying disciplined while everything around them screams. Hold the line, or you're moving back five yards.

> Defense wins championships. Backpressure wins uptime.

Autoscaling is your bench. It only helps if you called it up before the drive started — cold starts during a fourth-quarter surge are how you lose the game.
