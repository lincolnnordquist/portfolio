Every great crew has specialists — a navigator, a cook, a doctor — who each own one thing and trust the others to own theirs. A healthy distributed system is the same: services that do one job well and communicate through clear contracts instead of reaching into each other's kitchens.

## Consensus is a crew agreement

Raft and Paxos are just formalized ways for a crew to agree on which way the ship is pointing, even when messages get lost in a storm. The magic isn't the algorithm — it's accepting that some nodes will always be unreachable and building for it anyway.

> I don't want to conquer anything. I just want the whole system to stay up when one node goes down.

Set sail with idempotency, retries with backoff, and the humility to assume the network hates you. The Grand Line is just production with better weather effects.
